02 / About

Rooted in Mecca.
Built for the scroll.

I'm Reem AlMasri — a full-stack designer, video editor, and social media marketer. I move between identity systems, campaign art direction, illustration, and motion, so a brand can sound like itself everywhere it shows up.

My process starts with the audience: what they care about, what they'd share, and what makes them stop. Then I build the visual world around that — from tatreez-inspired identity systems to value-first content plans.

Six years in Photoshop, four in CapCut, and a soft spot for pomegranates.
